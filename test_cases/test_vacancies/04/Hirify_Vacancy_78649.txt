Job description
TL;DR
Data Architect: Designing data engineering solutions and overall data architecture with an accent on ETL/ELT strategy, data pipe mappings and leading the discovery process. Focus on creating high-level Data Architecture, explaining every part of it for more than one project at a time, and conducting all required interviews and choosing the right project data team members.
Location: Remote
Company
NDA is an international team: we have colleagues who work not only from the office but also remotely from all over the world.
What you will do
Planning the most proper solution design, partially designing Data Solution Architecture.
Designing data engineering solutions and writing high-quality documentation related to every project in each phase.
Designing ETL/ELT strategy: batch, streaming.
Creating data pipe mappings.
Leading overall discovery process related to data architecture for more than one project at a time.
Conducting all required interviews and choosing the right project data team members.
Requirements
Experience in SQL, Python, ETL/ELT, Data/Delta Lakes, DWH.
Knowledge of cloud platforms: AWS.
Good expertise in Spark and AWS data Services.
English: B2+
Analytical and problem-solving skills.
Nice to have
Knowledge of Azure, GCP.
Culture & Benefits
Competitive compensation.
Remote or office work and flexible working hours.
Healthcare benefits: medical insurance and paid sick leave.
Continuous education, mentoring, and professional development programs.
A team with excellent tech expertise.
Certifications paid by the company.
Be careful: if the employer asks you to log into their system using iCloud/Google, send codes/passwords, or run code/software, don't do it - these are scammers. Always click "Report" or contact support.
