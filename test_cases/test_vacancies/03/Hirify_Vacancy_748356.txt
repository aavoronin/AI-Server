TL;DR
Senior Data Engineer (Fintech): Designing and maintaining scalable data architecture and ETL pipelines for a fintech partner with an accent on GCP, BigQuery, and dbt. Focus on building robust data infrastructure, ensuring data quality, and collaborating with stakeholders to support analytical and operational decision-making.
Location: Remote (Global) or Hybrid in Vilnius, Lithuania.
Salary: €30–€40 per hour.
Company
hirify.global
is a software development agency providing outsourcing and staff augmentation services for clients across Lithuania and Europe.
What you will do
Design, build, and maintain the data warehouse and overall data architecture.
Create and optimize ETL/ELT pipelines for analytical and operational needs.
Implement integrations between internal platforms and third-party systems.
Establish data quality standards, governance practices, and monitoring routines.
Partner with data scientists and business stakeholders to ensure data availability.
Evolve data infrastructure using GCP, BigQuery, dbt, and Apache Superset.
Requirements
At least 3 years of hands-on experience in a Data Engineer role.
Solid working knowledge of GCP and BigQuery.
Practical experience using dbt for data modeling and transformations.
Strong Python and SQL skills.
Proven track record of building and tuning ETL/ELT pipelines.
Experience with Apache Superset or similar BI tools.
Nice to have
Previous experience in banking or fintech.
Culture & Benefits
Full-time, long-term engagement.
Preferred B2B freelance contract model with permanent employment options available.
Fully remote work flexibility.
Laptop and work equipment provided.
