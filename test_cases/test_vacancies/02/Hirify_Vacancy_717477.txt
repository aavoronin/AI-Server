TL;DR
Lead Data Engineer (Data Engineering): Lead the development of end-to-end data engineering initiatives by managing a team of data engineers and coordinating closely with analytics and product stakeholders with an accent on architecture design, production-grade pipelines, and workflow optimization. Focus on building and extending large-scale data systems using Apache Airflow, Spark, ClickHouse, dbt, Kafka, and production Python/SQL.
Location: Work remotely from all around the world
Company
hirify.global
is a European game developer and publisher with an internationally distributed team.
What you will do
Lead a team of data engineers and drive the development process end-to-end
Run team rituals (Weekly Planning, Daily Standups, Planning Poker)
Coordinate with analytics and product teams to deliver cross-functional outcomes
Design architecture for new data projects and support/extend existing systems
Optimize and automate development workflows
Requirements
Hands-on experience with Apache Airflow, Apache Spark, ClickHouse, dbt, Kafka
Production-level Python and SQL
Experience with object storage / data lakes (S3; Apache Iceberg is a plus)
Experience leading a team of 3–5 people or working as a tech lead with real ownership of deadlines
Ability to provide and maintain time estimates, plus strong communication and documentation/knowledge transfer skills
Understanding of product analytics and attribution (AppsFlyer, MyTracker, ad network trackers) is a strong plus
Culture & Benefits
Work remotely from all around the world
Collaborative internal game dev community across multiple studios
Access to specialists and expertise across different areas
Opportunity to experiment and work on ambitious tasks with strong resources
